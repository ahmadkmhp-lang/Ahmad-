# WO GALI (वो गली) — STORY MASTER LEDGER

Permanent record of every completed episode. Each entry lists the setting, core plot, and final twist so no future episode repeats them.

**Mandatory rule for every new episode (Ep 09 onward):**
1. Read this ledger before generating any new episode. The new story must not reuse any plot, setting, or twist listed here.
2. After the new episode is generated, append its 2–3 line entry at the bottom of this file in the same format, and push both files to `main`.

---

## Ep 01 — पापा की वीडियो कॉल (Papa ki Video Call)
- **Setting:** Family home at night, front door and a blue curtain inside the house.
- **Core Plot:** Papa video-calls late at night and warns the narrator not to open the door for the courier.
- **Final Twist:** Papa is already inside the house, standing behind the blue curtain, and he died three days ago.

## Ep 02 — दादी की चप्पल (Dadi ki Chappal)
- **Setting:** Bedroom at home at three AM.
- **Core Plot:** Dadi's slippers are heard walking at three AM, and a fake Dadi gives the rule to keep your eyes closed.
- **Final Twist:** Backward-turned feet are seen under the bed, while the real Dadi is away in the village.

## Ep 03 — लिफ्ट का आईना (Lift ka Aaina)
- **Setting:** Apartment lift on the fourteenth floor at eleven PM.
- **Core Plot:** An uncle in a white kurta with wet hair steps in and stands right behind the narrator, but the phone camera shows the lift is empty.
- **Final Twist:** The lift mirror shows him leaning on the narrator's shoulder with his neck twisted fully backward. The lift stops, the door stays shut, and he says they will get off together.

## Ep 04 — छत पर उल्टे कदम (Chhat par Ulte Kadam)
- **Setting:** Family house roof, locked for years with a rusty padlock, at one AM.
- **Core Plot:** Heavy footsteps and anklet jingles come from the roof every night. Papa breaks the lock and finds bare footprints in the dust that end in mid-air beyond the parapet.
- **Final Twist:** Behind them, the anklets start jingling on the stairs. Someone is slowly walking down into their house.

## Ep 05 — आखिरी मेट्रो का यात्री (Aakhri Metro)
- **Setting:** The last metro train of the night.
- **Core Plot:** A woman sitting opposite keeps staring and crying at the empty seat beside the narrator, then texts that an eyeless white face is resting on his shoulder.
- **Final Twist:** A second message says not to run because it never leaves the shoulder. Under a tube light, his shadow has a second head on its shoulder.

## Ep 06 — हॉस्टल का रूम नंबर दो सौ चार (Hostel Room 204)
- **Setting:** College hostel room 204 with a bunk bed, at three AM.
- **Core Plot:** Roommate Rohan on the upper bunk warns not to open the door because his lookalike is outside, and grips the narrator's wrist with an icy hand.
- **Final Twist:** The torch shows the real Rohan outside, crying and mouthing "run, that's not me". The one on the upper bunk is smiling and still holding the wrist.

## Ep 07 — बाथरूम के नल की आवाज (Raat Teen Baje ka Nal)
- **Setting:** Home bathroom at three AM with a fused bulb.
- **Core Plot:** For three nights the tap turns on by itself and the narrator tightens the cold, wet tap in the dark. Papa then reveals he had the tap removed the day before.
- **Final Twist:** Through the door crack, a black shadow hangs upside down from the ceiling, tapping the floor. One long finger is bent exactly where the tap used to be, and it beckons him inside.

## Ep 08 — पड़ोसी की खिड़की (Padosi ki Khidki)
- **Setting:** A young woman's rented fourth-floor city flat, facing the neighbour's balcony across a narrow lane, at midnight.
- **Core Plot:** An old aunty on the opposite balcony gestures "close your curtains" every night while staring past the narrator. Police break her door and find she died a week ago.
- **Final Twist:** From the aunty's balcony, the narrator sees a man standing behind her empty chair in her own room. The aunty was protecting her from him, and he looks across and slowly closes her curtains.

## Ep 11 — रसोई का बोलता स्पीकर (Bolta Speaker)
- **Setting:** A dark, empty kitchen in a family flat at two AM, with a smart speaker on the counter beside the fridge while the parents sleep.
- **Core Plot:** The smart speaker's LED ring lights up by itself and it says "I heard what you said", though Neha said nothing. The app's voice history shows that command logged on the next day's date at four thirteen AM, while it is still only two AM.
- **Final Twist:** In the recording, an unknown heavy voice calls Neha's name and her own voice answers "yes, I'm coming". The last shot jumps to four thirteen the next morning: the kitchen is empty, her phone lies on the floor, and the front door stands wide open.
